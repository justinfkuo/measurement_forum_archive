I was there at the start and finish. I was NOT responsible for mesurement, ...

jamesm #1 • Feb 12, 2005 at 09:28 PM
I was there at the start and finish. I was NOT responsible for mesurement, timing, clocks or the rulings.

I think the following report clears it up:

"Sonja Friend-Uhl of West Palm Beach, originally announced second in 1:19:05, was bumped to third when age-group runner Veena Reddy, 26, of Philadelphia had a faster time in 1:18:49"

I think Sonja came over the finish line second, followed by Reddy, who had started two minutes later.

Yes there was a special elite start, almost 2 minutes before the field.

There were two start guns. I beleve the time for each was calcualted from the time of their start gun. So I think the offset was factored into both gun and chip time.

The was almost two minutes between starts. I know the mats and starting systems were reset between starts.

Finish Chip Time
92 Debbi Kilpatrick ___ 1:18:32 1:18:31
10552 Veena Reddy __ 1:18:49 1:18:40
90 Sonja Friend Uhl __ 1:19:05 1:19:04

The two elite runners only took 1 second to cross the mats, paced after the start, where as Reddy, placed in the 10000+ pen, and started two minutes later, took 9 seconds. Looks like Reddy's finish time is calculated from the second start, not the first.

Last year there was a problem with clocks at the finish line showing the wrong time for some runners. This year that was cured by having two clocks on each finish line, one for elite and one for normal runners.
