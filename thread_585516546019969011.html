In compiling my annual report for the convention, I ran a statistic that I find interesting. I don't know how much value it has to anyone, but here goes:

We currently have 12,079 active courses, and a total of 29,272 courses. So 41.26% of all courses since 1979 are active ones.

Admin #2 • 11/27/07 @ 9:01 AM
How do you determine which courses are active? Or maybe a better question is, what is the definition of active?
peteriegel #3 • 11/27/07 @ 10:45 AM
Download the complete course list. See the column headed "status." Any course with a status beginning with "A" is active.

Active means that it has not expired, failed validation, or been replaced by another course.
