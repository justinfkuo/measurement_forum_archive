jhp #3 • 12/21/22 @ 6:30 PM

The review chain is from the measurer to the state certifier, to the Vice Chair East (Justin Kuo) or Vice Chair West (Jane Parks), then to the Registrar (Jane Parks).  The State Certifiers, Vice Chairs, and the Registrar are listed on the rrtc.net website with their email addresses. Please feel free to contact them if you need clarification on any comments from the on-line certification program.  Expanding the comment section is on the list of upgrades to the program but until that is changed please contact your certifier directly if you need more information about your submission.  Thank you.  Jane.
