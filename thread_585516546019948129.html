Interim Report – Course Measurement Bulletin Board

To all,

The Course Measurement Bulletin Board (https://measure.infopop.cc/eve/forums) has now been in operation for a week. Here is how we have fared:

We have 47 registered members.
26 messages have been posted.
800 page views have happened.

Most of the action took place in the first few days, with the weekend being in the doldrums. Things may be picking up this week.

Tell your friends. Growth, and new people posting messages, will help this become an effective and entertaining tool.

Some like to read more than to write. This is fine, but without messages there will be nothing to read. Messages need not be long – even a sentence expressing an opinion is of value.
