garybrumley #3 • 10/5/25 @ 4:32 AM

Ask your certifier to confirm, but here’s how I’d handle it:

Unless your mile 1 split is already certified, you can’t assume it is. Measure the segment from the start to mile 1 along the original route twice, then measure the same segment along the new route twice. Use the shorter of each pair of measurements to find the difference and adjust accordingly.

The “Seg Alt Tool” tab in my measurement spreadsheet will calculate this for you. Be sure to enter all your calibration ride data in the sheet for the formulas to work properly. The "About" tab provides instructions on how to use the document.

Once you’ve completed your calculations, submit everything to your certifier as an LDS submission (export the relevant data from the spreadsheet - Info tab, Cal tab, Seg Alt Tool tab - into PDF files and email to your certifier).

Admin #4 • 10/5/25 @ 7:12 AM

When doing an adjustment you must use the longer of your two measurements of the original course segment, and the shorter of your two measurements for the new course segment.
