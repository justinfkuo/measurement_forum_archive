Rio Marathon Course

From Pete Riegel:

I asked Rodolfo Eichler, Brazil’s top “A” measurer about elevations and he said:

"The main problem we were faced for this job was the urban renovation by the City, mainly in downtown area * see map w details. The course, as you know the area of Flamengo Park, runs along the Guanabara Bay and the renovated Port, with no significant elevation change Our visitors, measured it with poor traffic protection, could completed and adjust the distance, I myself and Suzana Gnaccarini, now herself “A” level measurer, acted as helpers with others escorts. David Katz, plenty of laser tools, will return Rio next 10th of August for the final battle; he is going to write on his impressions, RW – the 1000/2000 m, were designed 40km far from City Centre, zero elevation, it will be re certified on race days, always after the final location of cones and barriers (11th, 12th and 19th of August). *see the course map for a recent championship, I will be very happy to discuss all this course details with MNForum, about the three 10 km looping and site-seeing course, politically correct, but far from a technical one. So, the route is 3 loops and basically flat. Elevation very close to sea level throughout.
