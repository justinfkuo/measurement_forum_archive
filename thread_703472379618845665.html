The ChatGPT may become useful someday. For now, it's just for fun. For exam...

justinkuo #1 • Unknown date

The ChatGPT may become useful someday. For now, it's just for fun. For example, USATF Rules of Competition, Rule 240 Course Certification,  includes just three paragraphs. There is no USATF Rule 240.4, 240.5, 240.6, or 240.7.

Here's a fun example. I asked the A.I. to: Write a news article recapping the Boston Marathon, but all the runners are ducks.

Here's the A.I. generated response:

"Quackers Across the Finish Line: Ducks Take on the Boston Marathon"

Yesterday, the streets of Boston were filled with flapping wings and webbed feet as a group of determined ducks tackled the city's annual marathon.

Leading the pack was a plucky young mallard named Mary, who waddled her way to a first place finish with a time of 2 hours and 45 minutes. Close behind her was a team of four scaup, who crossed the finish line in a tight formation, quacking triumphantly as they earned second place.

But the true heroes of the race were the dozens of amateur ducks who took on the 26.2 mile course, some with the help of tiny wagons to carry their supplies. Spectators cheered and tossed bits of bread as the feathered athletes braved the heat and the hills, determined to reach the finish line.

In the end, all of the ducks were winners, as they proved that with a little determination and a lot of paddling, anything is possible. The Boston Marathon will never be the same without these quackers on the course.
