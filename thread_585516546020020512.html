Thanks to the efforts of Gene Newman and Bob Baumel, we now have an easier ...

bobthurston #1 • Unknown date
Thanks to the efforts of Gene Newman and Bob Baumel, we now have an easier way to find non-U.S. courses that have a USATF certification.

On the USATF site for course certifications, click on Additional Tools, then scroll down to Certified Course Lists. You will find a Foreign List, an Older Foreign List, and a Historical List. The foreign list has additional instructions at the top, explaining how to get the map for a course from its ID (the list has the course ID's).

I don't know how complete the foreign lists are. Some courses I've measured and certified overseas are missing, and I hope to get the information on those included. Others who have measured overseas may want to check to see if courses you've measured overseas are listed.

There has also been some good discussion about whether or not we should be doing USATF certifications for non-US courses. But apart from that question, we have done such courses and they have been hard to find on the search engine. Now, thanks to Gene and Bob B, there is a better way to find them.
