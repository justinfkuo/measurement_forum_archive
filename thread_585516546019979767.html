Of the currently active measurers in NC, Paul Hronjak has measured the most courses, 276 in his almost 30 years of measuring - my first four, starting in 1980, resulted in a brief notes from Ted Corbitt saying the course was certified. Actual certificates were not issued until Pete Riegel took over in 1983. Second on the list is Tim Rhodes with 171 courses to date (since 1996). Of course, the REAL leader is the late AC Linnerud who measured over 700 courses in his long career. I show Danny White with 77 but I'm sure his total is much higher than that since he has measured a lot of courses in SC as well. Let us know your real total, Danny. Also, Neville, for some reason I don't have your number for 2007. Please let us know. Without 2007, Neville has measured 83 courses since he started in 2001.
