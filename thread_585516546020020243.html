MEASUREMENT NEWS AVAILABLE ON DVD

Measurement News was the communication vehicle for course measurement from 1982 to 2004. It was published bimonthly and mailed to subscribers. Measurers and certifiers wrote articles and letters which were published for all to read. It's since been replaced by this online forum.

There’s a lot of history there, and answers to questions that keep arising. Also, you’ll get an insight into some of the measurers around the US and the world.

It is available at no cost by going to www.rrtc.net and looking for it at “Historical Archive on RunScore.com”
Unfortunately the online version’s index is not searchable.

I intend to sell DVD’s on the www.jonescounter.com website. It will have a searchable index.
Before I do, I’d like to get smarter about how to do it.

I can sell the DVD, in pdf format with searchable index, for $5, but am open to suggestions as to how best to make it available. I regard the searchable index as highly valuable, as it’s a long file, and a tedious job to use without a search function.

I find it quite handy to have the past issues in my computer rather than only online.

Any suggestions?
