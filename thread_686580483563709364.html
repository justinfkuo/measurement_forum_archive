New Measurer Search Map

Admin #1 • 1/27/22 @ 10:39 AM

The interactive map that can be used to see where course measurers are and get their contact information has been updated to include only measurers who have an account on certifiedroadraces.com and have selected the option to "Show Info on Measurers Page." The link to the map is below. The link is also available on the rrtc.net website.

http://www.rrtc.net/Measurers_map/measurers_map.html

Hover over or click on a dot to see measurer name and contact info. The locations of each measurer dot is near the center of their city, not their actual address. Zoom in to see multiple measurer dots in the same city.

If you notice incorrect location or info for a measurer let me know by posting here or sending me a private message. Thanks.
