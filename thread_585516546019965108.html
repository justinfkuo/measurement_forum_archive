One of the things I recall from the Old Days of MN was the Map of the Month. When measuring was in its infancy, it was always a fresh experience to see how maps were being done up. Now that MN is here, and I have 28,000+ maps in my basement, I'll share one a month. Each will be selected for either artistic layout, sheer volume of information, ease of reading, or just because I like it.

Frankly, I've seen some pretty nasty-looking maps lately. Perhaps the Map of the Month will inspire. A Thurston usually does it for me
