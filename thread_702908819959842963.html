Over the past few years there have been lots of great suggestions posted on...

Admin #1 • Unknown date

Over the past few years there have been lots of great suggestions posted on here for helpful phone and computer apps. I think it would be a good idea to summarize all those suggestions in a section on the RRTC.net website. One stop shopping!

With that in mind I have created a google sheet (I do everything with spreadsheets) for people to provide information about their favorite apps. You can even add a new category if you think of one.

https://docs.google.com/spread...SY0/edit?usp=sharing

I have given everyone with the link Editor access so you should be able to add your items (don't worry, I've made a copy of the file and will do so periodically, so I will be able to recover anything you might mess up). Make sure to include your name under "Name of Suggester" so I can contact you with questions. Finally, if you want to make additional comments about an app that has already been posted by someone else, add another line for the same app so we can know who made which comments.

After collecting all this info I will use it to create a new section on the RRTC.net website.

Thanks in advance for your help with this.
