I'm not sure this will help David Nash. Here, in the USATF New England Association, we have a Jones counter that is routinely rented out to folks interested in measuring and certifying their course withing our association. We include the counter, manual, video and a set of application papers. The cost of the one-week rental is $25. The association pays the shipping out, the measurer pays the return shipping. I'm would not be suprised if there are other USATF associations doing something similar.
