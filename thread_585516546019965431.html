Sounds good. But you may need to allow for robots searching the website. You can easily get half or more of the page views from search engine robots. As a check as to whether the board is indexed by Google I picked out the phrase "Strange calibration data" from the title of a May 10 posting and put it into Google with the quotes. This listed two sites and this board was one of them. So you are visited by at least the googlebot.
If you can access the server logs you can work out how many genuine individual viewers you have each month, but it may not be easy to do this.
