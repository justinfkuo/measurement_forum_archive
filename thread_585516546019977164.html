Dimension Survey

A cable-driven counter is in development. Some concern has been expressed that the cable may not be long enough to fit some bikes.

We are conducting a survey so we know what to design for.

Please measure your bike from the front wheel hub to the center of the handlebars at the handlebar mount. Measure the distance in a straight line. We will account for cable curvature

Send the length to Pete Riegel at riegelpete at aol dot com.

I will build a database from the information received, send the results to Tom (and post here). This will help us to properly plan the length or lengths of cable provided.

See below for the dimension desired.
