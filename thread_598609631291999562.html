You do not need to create an account to use the LEARN site -- in fact, the Settings link on LEARN has been deactivated.

On Sept 16, when the production site -- certifiedroadraces.com -- goes live, all users going to the site will be prompted to set up an account. 

Account holders who are also regional certifiers or final signatories will need to send an email to their Vice Chair requesting assignment to the appropriate status (certifier or FS) and their IAAF grade, where applicable.
