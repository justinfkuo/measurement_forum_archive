peteriegel #3 • 5/15/06 @ 4:31 AM
I present my bill when I present the certificate. I have only been stiffed once, when the race was cancelled. The customer is a friend and I am not sure he is even aware of missing what I considered to be his obligation.

I generally figure that if I get stiffed it's cash up front for any future work for that customer.

When payment is 30 days overdue I send a follow-up bill. This has always worked for me, and I am happy with my present method.
davepoppers #4 • 5/16/06 @ 10:12 AM
I have never been stiffed. Some have certainly been slow to pay but I now have on the invoice that a $10 penalty will be added if not paid in 30 days. I don't see any reason from my experience to require prepay except for a marathon, for instance, where a larger fee is to be paid. In that case I will expect possibly half the fee after a certain stage of work.

This did pay off for me a few years ago for the Denver International Marathon which did stiff the runners cash awards and the police, among others when the RD left in the dark of night (literally). By being in on the early activities, I'm sure the RD wasn't about to blow his reputation before the race.

Duane Russell of RaceMeasure is proving to be a super resource in Colorado as his work is very good and takes a lot of pressure off me. His packages are a pleasure to go through and I can stay less than overwhelmed for Colorado.
