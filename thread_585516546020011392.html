dougieroxburgh #11 • 5/23/14 @ 3:24 AM
Hi, Sorry for the late contribution; just noticed I was mentioned earlier on!
The UKCM app can store position information from the iPhone against logged waypoints but it does not store the track information. The feature allows logged points to be exported to a programme like Google Earth as a convenience when indulging in further mapping exploits. I can't vouch for the absolute accuracy of the iPhone location information as it is a bit unquantifiable. Consumer GPS units like the Garmin eTrex produce good track information (if maximum time-based updating used rather than heading-based updating) which is easily exportable to Google Earth. I usually restrict all of this data to the business of gross error checking and assistance in the production of the course plan.
