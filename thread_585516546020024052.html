Olympic Trials Map

genenewman #1 • Unknown date
For those interested, the course map is listed in the search engine as CA15078RS.

The first part is a 2.2 mile section, followed by a loop of 6 miles. This 6 mile section is run 4 times. Within the 6 mile loop, there is a long straight away where at each end some interesting turns on this loop. I feel if the race comes down the turns at the end it could be very interesting. Special thanks goes to our three IAAF measures for doing a great job: Ron Scardera, Doug Thurston and Mark Neal.

LA is having a heat wave and this could also be a factor. Enjoy the race.
