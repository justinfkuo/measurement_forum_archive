duanerussell #4 • 4/4/10 @ 4:55 PM
Thanks for the comments. I thought the interviewer asked some very intelligent questions. He is definitely a champion of certification.

Bob, I agree with you that life is too short to be designing courses while measuring. I have avoided much on-course tweaking by having directors use MMR. I have one in New Mexico that has told me on three different designs "The 8k is ready to measure", only to have me find it way short, as he didn't follow SPR. We now have one I can measure, and it will come out as we want. I think MMR is a great tool, especially for the "proof" of the course.

MapMyRun - Don't start a measurement without it!
