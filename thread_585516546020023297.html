For the Ted Corbitt Award, this was explained in Jim Gilmer's report in the document Library. Here is the report:
Committee Report on the RRTC Ted Corbitt Award At the 2014 annual meeting, and grounded in discussions from prior years’ meetings, the Council resolved to institute an award under the auspices of USATF that would honor and commemorate Ted Corbitt by recognizing those individuals who have made significant contributions and/or rendered substantial service to the sport through activities associated with the procedure, practice and administration of course measurement in road running. With the initial presentation of the RRTC Ted Corbitt Award slated for the 2015 annual meeting, Council Chair Gene Newman appointed a committee to establish criteria and standards for the award and to forward the slate of nominees for the inaugural award. This committee was coordinated by At-Large Member Jim Gilmer and also included Validations Chair Mike Wickiser and Final Signatory Certifier Carol McLatchie, as well as Council Chair Gene Newman.
The committee met monthly from March through June and after much discussion of various methods and procedures for selecting and vetting nominees decided for purposes of the 2015 inauguration that the criterion for receiving the Ted Corbitt Award should focus on the “foundational contributions to the institution and development of the Council.” Adoption of additional standards and criteria governing the nomination and selection of candidates for this award in subsequent years was relegated to the Corbitt Award Committee agenda for 2016.
Using the “foundational contributions” criterion, the following individuals were unanimously selected and endorsed by the committee to receive the first annual RRTC Ted Corbitt Award: • Tom and Mary Anne McBrayer • Pete Riegel • Wayne and Sally Nicoll • Paul Hronjak
Respectfully submitted,
Jim Gilmer At-Large Member, RRTC
